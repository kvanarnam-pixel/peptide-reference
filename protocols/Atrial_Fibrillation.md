# Atrial Fibrillation — Tissue Overlay

## The system

The failure that produces AFib is electrical and structural — atrial remodeling, stretch, fibrosis, sleep apnea, alcohol, thyroid, and electrolyte chaos. Rate control, rhythm control, and stroke-risk decisions belong with cardiology.

This page is a narrower overlay: inflammatory tone and mitochondrial strain in a high-ATP organ that has already been remodeled. It is not a rhythm drug.

## Category one — inflammatory / endothelial neighborhood

**KPV** — quieter NF-κB-type tone in an inflamed atrium. Mechanism, not cardioversion.

**BPC-157** — repair coordinator. Cardiac conversation in the literature is mechanistic / preclinical-heavy. Not an anti-arrhythmic.

## Category two — mitochondria in a high-ATP organ

**SS-31** — cardiolipin / electron-transport protection. Heart muscle qualifies. No AFib-outcome trial.

**MOTS-c** — metabolic / fuel-flexibility backdrop. Support. Not a rate-control agent.

## Category three — remodeling access (support, thin)

**TB-500** — access / anti-fibrotic conversation in the source notes. Thin for a public arrhythmia page. Named, not centered.

## Oral note that must stay careful

**Nattokinase** sits in a clot / fibrin conversation in the source papers. It is **not** a substitute for prescribed anticoagulation. Do not stop warfarin, a DOAC, or aspirin because this name exists.

Magnesium glycinate, potassium status, omega-3, and D-ribose are the less-loaded orals on this map.

## Markers people track

- ECG / wearable burden — clinician-owned
- TSH
- Potassium and magnesium
- Sleep-apnea screen
- hs-CRP if a systemic inflammatory picture is also live

## Evidence tier

Cardiology first.  
**KPV / BPC-157 / SS-31 / MOTS-c** — inflammatory + mitochondrial overlay. No AFib outcomes trial of this set.

## What this does not cover

Stroke-risk decisions, cardioversion, ablation timing, or stopping prescribed cardiac drugs.

## Related compounds

KPV, BPC-157, SS-31, MOTS-c, TB-500. Doses live on those cards.
