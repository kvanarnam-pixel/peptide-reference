# Shingles / VZV Support

## The system

The failure is VZV waking up when cellular immunity slips, then leaving inflamed and sometimes neuropathic tissue behind. Standard care is antivirals early. This page is an immune and repair overlay next to that care.

Do not delay antivirals for this list. Eye involvement and disseminated zoster exit this page.

## Category one — T-cell conversation (primary adjunct)

**Thymosin Alpha-1** — T-cell / innate-precision job while a latent herpesvirus is active. Adjunct language only. Not an antiviral.

## Category two — local repair and quieting

**BPC-157** — tissue repair coordinator at a beaten-up dermatome.

**TB-500** — access / cell-motility crew when there is actual tissue to reach.

**KPV** — inflammatory / pain-site quieting. Does not kill virus.

## Category three — recovery energy / redox (support)

**NAD+** — cellular energy during a metabolically expensive illness.

**Glutathione** — redox support. No card yet (placeholder). Same job language as the liver and hypertension pages. Not an antiviral.

## Named, thinner, not default

**LL-37** — antimicrobial-peptide conversation in the source notes. No card (placeholder). Not first-line next to a nucleoside antiviral. Named so it is not silently dropped.

**Nicotine** — in the source notes again. Not a default VZV tool.

## Markers people track

None required from the peptide map. Immunosuppression workup if recurrences stack up.

## Evidence tier

Antivirals first.  
**TA-1** — immune adjunct logic.  
**BPC / TB / KPV** — repair + quieting after the skin is already involved.  
**LL-37 / glutathione** — named jobs, thinner public evidence for this indication.

No combined human “peptide shingles protocol” trial.

## What this does not cover

Ophthalmic zoster, disseminated disease, or treating post-herpetic neuralgia as if it were simple shingles.

## Related compounds

Thymosin Alpha-1, BPC-157, TB-500, KPV, NAD+. Glutathione and LL-37 are placeholders. Doses live on the cards that exist.
