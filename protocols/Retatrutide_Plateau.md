# Retatrutide Plateau

## The system

Appetite and nutrient-partitioning drugs change intake. They do not automatically fix thyroid conversion, inflammatory drag, or mitochondrial output. A stall is often several of those at once — adaptive thermogenesis (the body spending less as mass falls), residual cytokine tone, and an energy system that cannot use the fuel that is still coming in.

This page is the map *around* a Retatrutide signal that has flattened. It is not a dose-escalation guide, and it is not a claim that adding peptides restarts fat loss.

Food quality, protein, training, sleep, and the Retatrutide card still do more of this job than anything below.

## What actually sits at the center

**Retatrutide** is already the metabolic accelerator. GLP-1 / GIP / glucagon signaling is doing intake, partitioning, and visceral-fat work. If that signal is present and the scale or waist has stopped moving, the useful question is which *other* job is the bottleneck — not whether a higher milligram is automatically the next move. Escalation decisions live on the Retatrutide card and with a clinician.

## Category one — energy machinery that may be the stall (primary add)

**MOTS-c** maps here the same way it does on the insulin-resistance page: AMPK / fuel-flexibility / insulin-independent glucose handling. A body that has lost mass and is now defending its weight often looks metabolically stiff. That is a programming job, not a second copy of Retatrutide.

**SS-31** is the membrane that program runs on — cardiolipin / electron-transport protection. Pairing MOTS-c with SS-31 is the same mechanistic 1-2 punch used elsewhere in this app. It is not a combined human “break a GLP-agonist plateau” trial.

## Category two — the carrier and the drag (primary when those are the story)

**NAD+** is the charge carrier those pathways use. Biochemistry is real. Injectable NAD+ as a proven plateau-breaker is not. Support for category one, not a third metabolic drug.

**KPV** belongs when inflammatory tone is still taxing the system — a stall with a loud hs-CRP, a gut that never settled, or a “everything is puffy and expensive” picture. Quiet the drag. It does not move GLUT4.

## Category three — repair backdrop (support)

**BPC-157** is the least specific name on this page. The source recipes put it next to a stall as general repair support. The job it *can* do is quieter tissue and barrier noise while the metabolic work is happening. It is not a weight-loss peptide. Cofactor, not a sixth mechanism.

## Named in the source notes — not on the public list

**5-Amino-1MQ** is an NNMT inhibitor (a small molecule, already in this app as its own card). The job is NAD+ preservation inside fat tissue. Real mechanism, different confidence than MOTS-c on this page, and easy to stack just because both words say “NAD.” Left off the public pairing so this page does not become a second metabolic-liver stack.

## Markers people track

Weight alone is a blunt instrument.

- Waist and a strength or training log, not only the scale
- Fasting insulin and fasting glucose together
- Triglycerides and HDL
- Free T3 and reverse T3 if a clinician is already asking about conversion
- hs-CRP when inflammatory drag is the live question

A stall with falling strength and low protein intake is a different problem than a stall with a flat waist and high inflammatory tone.

## Oral supports that actually map

- Magnesium glycinate — energy-system cofactor
- Selenium and zinc — thyroid-conversion conversation if that is actually in play
- Omega-3 — inflammatory backdrop
- Ubiquinol / CoQ10 — electron-transport companion to SS-31
- Curcumin / quercetin — thinner, optional, same inflammatory neighborhood as KPV

## Evidence tier

**Retatrutide** — human metabolic outcome data exists on its own card. That is not a trial of “Reta plus this list.”  
**MOTS-c / SS-31** — Tier 2 pathway logic imported from the insulin-resistance map. No plateau-specific human pairing.  
**NAD+ / KPV / BPC-157** — support jobs; weakest as standalone stall tools.

No combined human trial of this overlay exists.

## What this does not cover

Climbing the Retatrutide dose because the calendar says so. Muscle loss from under-eating. Gallbladder symptoms. “Eat less forever.” Hypothyroidism workup. Those belong on the Retatrutide card and in clinic care.

## Related compounds

Retatrutide, MOTS-c, SS-31, NAD+, KPV, BPC-157. Doses and cycling live on those cards.
