# Hypothyroid & Autoimmune Thyroid Support

## The system

The failure is not one number. Low free T3, high reverse T3, and TPO / thyroglobulin antibodies are different problems that get lumped as “thyroid.” Deiodinases actually use selenium and zinc. Iron and cortisol change conversion. Peptides here are adjunct conversation around conversion and inflammatory tone.

This page does not replace levothyroxine or a full thyroid workup when hormone is indicated.

## Category one — conversion and cofactors (the honest center)

The center is labs and cofactors, not an injectable.

Selenium and zinc belong here because the enzymes that convert T4 → T3 use them. Ferritin belongs here because conversion and hair/energy overlap when iron is actually low. Those live on the oral list.

## Category two — inflammatory / immune tone (when antibodies are the story)

**KPV** — NF-κB-type quieting when the gland is an inflammatory neighborhood.

**Thymosin Alpha-1** — immune-modulating conversation in autoimmune notes. Real job on paper. Not a thyroid-hormone replacement. Not a TPO-antibody trial.

**BPC-157** — repair / inflammatory coordination. Support. Not a thyroid peptide.

## Category three — energy machinery (support)

**MOTS-c** and **NAD+** — metabolic / mitochondrial backdrop when the complaint is “I converted poorly and I have no fuel.” They do not make T3.

## Markers people track

- TSH, Free T4, Free T3, Reverse T3
- TPO / thyroglobulin antibodies
- Ferritin
- Morning cortisol if conversion and HPA are both in play

## Oral supports that actually map

- Selenium
- Zinc
- Magnesium glycinate
- NAC
- Ubiquinol
- Iron bisglycinate with vitamin C — only if iron is actually low
- Methylated B-complex

## Evidence tier

Orals and replacement hormone (when indicated) do the thyroid job.  
**KPV / TA-1 / BPC-157** — inflammatory / immune overlay.  
**MOTS-c / NAD+** — energy support.

No combined human “reverse Hashimoto’s” trial. This page will not use that language.

## What this does not cover

Myxedema, pregnancy thyroid management, or stopping prescribed thyroid hormone.

## Related compounds

KPV, Thymosin Alpha-1, BPC-157, MOTS-c, NAD+. Doses live on those cards.
