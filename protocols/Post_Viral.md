# Post-Viral / Long-Haul Support

## The system

The failure after some infections is not the virus still replicating. It is leftover immune noise, mitochondrial debt, and a lining or nerve bed that did not reset. Fatigue, POTS-like pulse, brain fog, and a gut that never settled get dumped in the same bucket. They are not one disease.

This page is adjunct conversation after the acute infection is over. It is not an antiviral and not a COVID treatment.

## Category one — immune precision (primary when the leftover is immune)

**Thymosin Alpha-1** — T-cell / innate-precision job. Fits “I never got my immune bookkeeping back.” Not a standing order for every tired month after a cold.

## Category two — inflammatory leftover

**KPV** — NF-κB-type quieting when the leftover is cytokine chatter (hs-CRP, reactive lining, “everything flares”).

## Category three — energy debt

**MOTS-c** and **SS-31** — the same energy pair used on the metabolic pages. Post-exertional crashes are often an energy-failure story.  
**NAD+** — carrier. Support.

## Named if the lining is the leftover

**BPC-157** — gut–lung–barrier repair conversation when the post-viral picture is a lining that never closed. Not default if the only leftover is fatigue.

## Markers people track

hs-CRP, ferritin (acute-phase vs iron), TSH / Free T3, fasting insulin, B12. POTS-style workup if the pulse is the story. Do not invent a “long-haul peptide panel.”

## Oral supports that actually map

- Magnesium glycinate
- Omega-3
- Vitamin D3
- NAC / glycine
- Electrolytes if orthostatic symptoms are live

## Evidence tier

**TA-1 / KPV / MOTS-c / SS-31** — immune + quieting + energy. Mechanism overlay. No combined human long-haul trial of this set.

## What this does not cover

Acute infection, hypoxia, myocarditis workup, or calling this an antiviral protocol.

## Related compounds

Thymosin Alpha-1, KPV, MOTS-c, SS-31, NAD+, BPC-157. Doses live on those cards.
