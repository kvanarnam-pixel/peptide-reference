# Chronic Stress & High Cortisol

## The system

The failure is an HPA axis that will not stand down — cortisol as a 24-hour feature instead of a morning tool — and the inflammatory wake that follows it. Sleep, training load, alcohol, and light do more of this job than any peptide list.

This page is not a Cushing protocol and not a hydrocortisone substitute.

## What actually sits at the center

The center is not a peptide. It is load: light, alcohol, late training, unread recovery. Peptides here are adjunct conversation around that.

## Category one — night-side architecture, only if sleep is the broken piece (support)

**DSIP** is centered on the insomnia page. It belongs here only when the stress picture includes failed slow-wave sleep — the night cannot land, so the next day’s HPA stays loud. If sleep is intact and the problem is daytime drive, leave DSIP off.

Do not run this as a second copy of the insomnia map.

## Category two — immune / inflammatory wake (thin)

**Thymosin Alpha-1** already has a card. Source papers put it next to chronic stress as an immune-modulating / HPA-adjacent note. The job is real in immunosenescence and infection-recovery conversations. As a cortisol tool it is thin. Named because the immune drain of chronic stress is part of the system failure — not because TA-1 lowers cortisol in a trial.

**BPC-157** is background repair / stress-inflammation conversation. Same thinness. Cofactor, not an HPA drug.

## Category three — the mapped orals are doing more work than the injectables

This is the rare page where the oral list is the honest center of what people actually use:

- Phosphatidylserine — night-side cortisol convention
- Magnesium glycinate
- Taurine
- Ashwagandha — cycled in the source notes; not hydrocortisone
- Rhodiola — daytime drive; can be wrong if the person is already wired

No doses on this page.

## Markers people track

- Morning cortisol, or a clinician-ordered 24-hour urinary free cortisol
- A single random cortisol is a poor map
- Sleep and training log — often more informative than the lab

## Evidence tier

Lifestyle first.  
**DSIP** — sleep-architecture job, borrowed from the insomnia page when that job is live.  
**Thymosin Alpha-1 / BPC-157** — Tier 3 on this page: real compounds, weak cortisol-specific evidence.

No combined human “HPA reset” trial.

## What this does not cover

Cushing workup, steroid-dependent disease, adrenal “fatigue” as a diagnosis, or using ashwagandha as if it were a glucocorticoid.

## Related compounds

DSIP (only if sleep is broken), Thymosin Alpha-1, BPC-157. Doses live on those cards.
