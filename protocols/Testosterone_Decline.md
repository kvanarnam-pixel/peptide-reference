# Age-Related Testosterone Decline

## The system

The failure can sit in three places: hypothalamus (pulse generator), pituitary (LH / FSH), or testis (the factory). Different tools poke different levels. Sleep apnea, visceral fat, alcohol, and opioids flatten this axis more often than a missing peptide.

This page is HPG-axis support conversation. It is not a TRT protocol and not a fertility protocol.

## Category one — upstream pulse (support)

**Kisspeptin** — GnRH-pulse conversation. No card in this app yet (placeholder, same rule as menopause). Source papers used a pulse schedule; that timing is convention. Upstream only. It does not replace a testis that has quit.

## Category two — LH-receptor stand-in (support, store-sensitive)

**hCG** — LH-receptor agonist language. No card yet (placeholder). Frame it only as an HPG / testicular-stimulus conversation. Do **not** frame hCG as a weight-loss product.

## Category three — endogenous T via SERM (oral, named)

**Enclomiphene** — SERM conversation for keeping endogenous production online. Oral, not a peptide. No card. Named because the job is actually “ask the axis to keep working,” which is a different failure than injecting testosterone.

## Category four — GH-axis neighbors (different job)

**CJC-1295** and **Ipamorelin** already have cards. They do **not** raise testosterone by poking GnRH, LH, or the testis. CJC is a GHRH analog (duration). Ipamorelin is a GHS-R1a agonist (pulse amplitude). Together they are the bedtime GH stack already named in this app.

The only honest link to a low-T picture is indirect:

- The largest natural GH pulse sits in slow-wave sleep — the same night window where overnight testicular recovery is supposed to happen
- Visceral fat and poor sleep flatten T more often than a missing secretagogue
- Source notes paired them at night with a T stack as clinic convention, not as an HPG trial

Use them if the system failure includes broken sleep architecture or a GH-axis body-comp job. Do not add them to “complete” kisspeptin / hCG / enclomiphene. That would be two axes on one page pretending to be one job.

Tesamorelin is another GHRH analog. Same receptor family as CJC. Do not stack Tesamorelin + CJC on this page.

## Markers people track

- Total and free testosterone
- LH, FSH
- Estradiol
- Prolactin when indicated
- Hematocrit if androgens are in play
- Sleep-apnea screen and waist — often the actual cause

## Oral supports that actually map

- Zinc
- Vitamin D3 and K2
- Selenium
- Magnesium glycinate
- B12 / folate

## Evidence tier

Labs and the boring causes first.  
**Kisspeptin / hCG / enclomiphene** — level-specific HPG tools.  
**CJC-1295 / Ipamorelin** — GH-axis tools. Indirect neighbors via sleep and body comp. Not T secretagogues.

No combined human “peptide TRT alternative” trial.

## What this does not cover

Anabolic cycles, fertility planning details, stopping prescribed TRT, or ignoring apnea / obesity as the reason T is low.
