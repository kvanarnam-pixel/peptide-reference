# Small-Fiber & Idiopathic Neuropathy Support

## The system

The failure is small axons — pain, temperature, autonomic fibers — that are metabolically expensive and poorly supplied. Diabetic neuropathy already owns the glucose-driven version of this story (and ARA-290’s human pain-trial language lives there). This tile is the same tissue when the cause is not “known diabetic,” or not yet.

Workup still matters: glucose, B12, SPEP, thyroid, autoimmune, B6 excess, HIV / hep, toxins.

## Category one — innate-repair / small-fiber conversation

**ARA-290** — innate-repair-receptor ligand. The human signal that exists is in painful diabetic neuropathy, not in every idiopathic small-fiber clinic. Import that job here as “same tissue, different label,” not as a completed idiopathic trial.

## Category two — supply and structure

**BPC-157** — repair coordinator.  
**TB-500** — access / microvascular conversation around ischemic nerve.  
**GHK-Cu** — matrix / epithelial remodeling.

Same trio as the diabetic-neuropathy page. Do not run a second different stack just because the A1c is 5.6.

## Category three — energy in long axons (support)

**MOTS-c / SS-31 / NAD+** — mitochondrial program in tissue that cannot afford energy failure. Support, same as other nerve pages.

## Markers people track

A1c / time-in-range even when “not diabetic,” B12, TSH, SPEP when a clinician would order it, skin-biopsy IENFD if that test is already in play. This page does not invent a peptide marker.

## Oral supports that actually map

- Alpha-lipoic acid
- Benfotiamine
- Methylated B-complex (watch B6)
- Omega-3
- Magnesium

## Evidence tier

**ARA-290** — human data live on the diabetic page; extrapolation here.  
**BPC / TB / GHK** — supply and structure.  
**MOTS-c / SS-31** — energy support.

No combined human idiopathic-small-fiber trial of this overlay.

## What this does not cover

Acute foot infection, cauda equina, or skipping the boring workup because a peptide list exists.

## Related compounds

ARA-290, BPC-157, TB-500, GHK-Cu, MOTS-c, SS-31. Doses live on those cards.
