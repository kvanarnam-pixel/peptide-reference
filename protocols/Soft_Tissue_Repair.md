# Soft-Tissue Repair

## The system

The failure is a tendon, ligament, fascia, or muscle-tendon junction that is not finishing repair — load exceeded biology. Collagen is slow. Blood supply to tendon is worse than muscle. This is the job BPC-157, TB-500, and GHK-Cu were actually built to discuss.

This page is tissue-repair conversation. It is not a substitute for a torn-structure MRI, a surgeon, or load management.

## Category one — the foreman (primary)

**BPC-157** — local repair coordinator. Angiogenesis in injured tissue, growth-factor neighborhood, gut-and-soft-tissue research conversation. The center of this map. Oral vs injectable and “near the site” convention live on the BPC card, not as a dose here.

## Category two — the access crew (primary when the site is stuck)

**TB-500** (Thymosin Beta-4 fragment) — cell motility, actin, getting cells to the job site, anti-fibrotic conversation. Complementary to BPC on paper: one coordinates, one improves access. Not a second copy of category one.

## Category three — materials and matrix (support)

**GHK-Cu** — copper-binding tripeptide, matrix remodeling, gene-expression reset language. Materials-and-blueprints next to the foreman. Fits when the problem is scar quality / remodeling, not just “make it less angry.”

## When the site is still loud

**KPV** — NF-κB quieting if the tendon or fascia is an inflammatory neighborhood. Leave it off a quiet, chronic, under-loaded tendon.

## Markers people track

Function and load tolerance, not a peptide lab. Imaging if a complete tear is on the table.

## Oral supports that actually map

- Vitamin C
- Glycine, proline, lysine
- Copper (GHK is a copper peptide; copper status is not a trivia note)
- Zinc
- Collagen + training — the stimulus is still the job

## Evidence tier

**BPC-157 / TB-500 / GHK-Cu** — complementary repair jobs. Human sports-medicine outcome trials of the stack are not the foundation. Preclinical and clinic convention are.

No combined human “Wolverine protocol” trial. This page will not use that nickname as a claim.

## What this does not cover

Complete rupture, infection in a joint, or dropping PT because a peptide list exists.

## Related compounds

BPC-157, TB-500, GHK-Cu. KPV only if the site is inflamed. Doses live on those cards.
