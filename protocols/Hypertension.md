# Hypertension

## The system

The failure people blame on “high pressure” is usually afterload + kidneys + sleep apnea + salt + vessel tone. Peptides cannot replace that list. What they can be mapped to is a narrower slice: vascular inflammatory tone and endothelial redox — the lining of the vessel that is supposed to make nitric oxide and keep the wall from staying angry.

This page is that overlay. It is not a blood-pressure drug.

## Category one — endothelial repair conversation (primary peptide job)

**BPC-157** — VEGFR2 / eNOS / organized angiogenesis language in the research conversation. That is an endothelial job, not a diuretic. Preclinical and mechanism-heavy. It does not replace an ACE inhibitor.

## Category two — vascular inflammatory quieting (primary when the wall is loud)

**KPV** — NF-κB-type signaling in an inflamed endothelium. Same quieting job as other pages, aimed at the vessel instead of the gut. Fits a picture with high inflammatory tone. Does not lower afterload.

## Category three — the redox buffer the endothelium spends (support — bigger job than a footnote)

Endothelial nitric-oxide synthase uncouples when oxidative load is high. That is a glutathione / NADPH-redox job, not a peptide-receptor job.

**Glutathione** is the buffer. Oral precursors (NAC, glycine) already do part of this work. Injectable glutathione showed up in the source recipes as a direct-GSH move. There is no glutathione card in this app yet — placeholder, same rule as the liver page — but the *job* is central to this system failure, not an optional note. Do not read this as “IV glutathione is an antihypertensive.”

## Does a thinner stack finish the job?

For the slice this page is allowed to own — angry endothelium + spent redox buffer — **BPC-157 + KPV + glutathione** covers the two peptide jobs and the redox job. Adding MOTS-c or SS-31 would import the metabolic-mitochondrial map without a hypertension-outcome reason. Left off.

Magnesium, potassium, addressing apnea, and prescribed antihypertensives still do more for the number on the cuff.

## Markers people track

- Home BP series, not one reading
- Creatinine / eGFR
- Electrolytes
- Sleep-apnea screen

## Oral supports that actually map

- Magnesium glycinate
- Omega-3
- Vitamin D3 and vitamin K2
- Zinc
- Ubiquinol
- NAC / glycine — glutathione precursors

**Not on this page:** nattokinase. It sits in a clot conversation. It is not a substitute for prescribed anticoagulation and it does not belong on a public blood-pressure tile.

## Evidence tier

**BPC-157 / KPV** — endothelial / inflammatory mechanism. No hypertension RCT.  
**Glutathione** — established redox biochemistry; not an antihypertensive outcome trial.

No combined human trial. Do not stop a CCB, ACE inhibitor, or diuretic because this list exists.

## What this does not cover

Hypertensive emergency, pregnancy hypertension, medication changes, or “peptides instead of addressing apnea.”

## Related compounds

BPC-157, KPV. Glutathione is named as a central support job; card not built. Doses live on the cards that exist.
