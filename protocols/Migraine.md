# Migraine

## The system

Migraine is not one switch. The failure that keeps showing up is expensive neurons that cannot keep up with demand — energy failure in susceptible cortex — plus inflammatory sensitization of the trigeminal system, plus (for some people) a gut–brain background that never went quiet. Magnesium, riboflavin, and membrane fats show up in the same conversation because they sit on that energy-and-membrane job.

This page maps those overlapping jobs. It is not an abortive. It does not replace a triptan, a CGRP drug, or a red-flag workup.

## Category one — neuronal energy failure (primary)

**MOTS-c** maps here for AMPK / fuel-flexibility. If the attacks track under-fueling, sleep debt, or metabolic stiffness, this is the peptide with a coherent job. It is not a pain drug.

**SS-31** is imported from the same energy map used elsewhere in this app: cardiolipin / electron-transport protection in high-ATP tissue. Neurons qualify. There is no migraine-outcome trial. Membrane support under the MOTS-c program, not a second abortive.

## Category two — inflammatory sensitization (primary when that is the story)

**KPV** interrupts NF-κB-type signaling. Source notes timed it to prodrome (light aversion). That timing is convention, not a trial design. The job is quieter inflammatory tone in a system that is already too easy to set off. It does not abort an attack that has already peaked.

## Category three — gut–brain / repair backdrop (support)

**BPC-157** is not a migraine peptide. The job it *can* do is the wider inflammatory and barrier background — especially when migraine sits on IBS, food triggers, or a lining that never settled. Foreman on that backdrop. Weakest of the four as a standalone headache tool.

## Markers people track

Diagnosis and red flags stay with a clinician. From this map:

- RBC magnesium
- B-vitamin status if deficiency is plausible (riboflavin lives on the oral list for a reason)
- hs-CRP only if a systemic inflammatory picture is also in play

A sudden “worst headache,” aura with focal signs, or a new pattern after 50 exits this page.

## Oral supports that actually map

These have thicker human-use history than the peptides for this complaint — still not a prescription:

- Magnesium (threonate or glycinate)
- Riboflavin
- CoQ10 / ubiquinol
- Omega-3
- Methylene blue — named in the source papers as an ETC-bypass oral; hormetic, MAOI interaction, pharmaceutical grade only. Optional, not a default add

## Evidence tier

**MOTS-c / SS-31** — Tier 2 energy-pathway logic. No migraine pairing trial.  
**KPV** — inflammatory job; prodrome timing is convention.  
**BPC-157** — support / gut–brain.

No combined human migraine trial of this overlay. Orals (mag / riboflavin / CoQ10) are the part of this conversation with the most ordinary human use data.

## What this does not cover

Focal neuro signs, thunderclap headache, medication-overuse headache, untreated apnea, or cervical / visual triggers. Do not drop a prescribed preventative because this list exists.

## Related compounds

MOTS-c, SS-31, KPV, BPC-157. Doses live on those cards.
