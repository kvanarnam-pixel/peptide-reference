## Identity and evidence boundary

**CJC-1295**, **CJC-1295 DAC**, and **DAC:GRF** are the same object: tetrasubstituted GHRH(1–29) plus an Nε-maleimidopropionyl-lysine (DAC) that covalently bioconjugates to Cys34 of albumin.

**Modified GRF 1-29** (“CJC-1295 no DAC”) is the tetrasubstituted 29-mer without that tail. Community naming taped the famous name onto the short analog. This file does not treat those as interchangeable.

Governing maxim: **mechanism earns inclusion; evidence type determines wording; the wrong molecule cannot borrow the right paper.**

Evidence layers that matter here — keep these separate:

1. **Direct human CJC-1295 DAC data** — Teichman 2006 JCEM PK/PD; Ionescu & Frohman 2006 JCEM overnight pulsatility; Sackmann-Sala 2009 proteomic follow-on. Healthy adults. Hormone endpoints. Weeks, not years. No body-composition primary endpoint. No dedicated discontinuation / rebound trial.
2. **GHRH-receptor desensitization from non-human / cell work** — biologically plausible adaptation under sustained GHRH tone. Not a human DAC week-12 curve. Do not promote it to “the receptor genuinely turns itself down, therefore 12/4 exists.”
3. **GHRH + GHS / ghrelin-receptor synergy physiology** — Cunha & Mayo 2002 and the older two-door literature. Supports pairing language. Does not create a named CJC+Ipa human trial.
4. **Practitioner / community dosing and cycling convention** — 1–2 mg weekly, bedtime, 12/4, live-card supplement lists. Keep visible. Do not promote them to trial-derived.
5. **Class / cousin data** — tesamorelin GHRH-analog RCTs (including 2-week withdrawal of GH pulses and IGF-1 toward baseline; VAT return after stop). Somatropin labels for malignancy, retinopathy, apnea / fluid caution. Different molecules. Useful as climate caution, not as silent DAC fill.
6. **Development history** — ConjuChem Phase II HIV-lipodystrophy hold after an on-study death. See regulatory note. Not a safety rate. Not a clearance.

### Dose and cycling — evidence sourcing

Teichman tested single subcutaneous doses of 30, 60, 125, and 250 µg/kg, then weekly or biweekly repeats in a second short study. Better tolerated at 30–60 µg/kg in that study. Those µg/kg figures do not automatically become the live-card milligram. Live card remains **1–2 mg once weekly** this pass.

After a single dose: mean GH approximately 2- to 10-fold for ≥6 days; mean IGF-1 approximately 1.5- to 3-fold for 9–11 days (IGF-1 persistence also described out toward 14 days in the same paper’s longer look). Half-life 5.8–8.1 days. Drug still measurable 10–13 days. After multiple weekly or biweekly doses: cumulative IGF-1 effect; mean IGF-1 still above baseline up to ~28 days in that short window. Do not quote the 2- to 10-fold figure as IGF-1.

Ionescu & Frohman used 60 or 90 µg/kg once, then sampled overnight a week later. Trough GH about 7.5-fold; mean GH +46%; IGF-1 +45%; pulse frequency and peak height not the story.

12 weeks on / 4 weeks off is practitioner-community convention. No DAC-specific human desensitization curve at that calendar was identified.

### Washout — evidence sourcing

No published human study was built to map DAC-CJC discontinuation, rebound-below-baseline, or persistence of body-composition change.

What can be said from this molecule: PK half-life ~6–8 days; GH elevation after one dose ~6+ days; IGF-1 longer than GH; repeated dosing leaves IGF-1 up into the 2–4 week range of that short study. A four-week off stretch is therefore in the neighborhood of drug clearance plus downstream lag, not a proven “fully baseline on day 28.”

Cousin: Stanley et al. tesamorelin 2 mg daily × 2 weeks in healthy men — GH secretion parameters and IGF-1 back near baseline after 2 weeks off. Tesamorelin Phase III — VAT largely returns after stop; IGF-1 falls toward baseline when the analog stops. Exogenous GH withdrawal literature shows IGF-1 returning to baseline rather than a documented crash below it; body-fat rebound can overshoot in some GH-discontinuation settings. None of that is a DAC rebound trial. Tier 1 therefore says downward drift, no proven crash, no souvenir guarantee.

### Response-tracking evidence sourcing

IGF-1 is a downstream marker the papers actually measured. A rise proves the axis moved. It does not prove the reason you bought the vial.

### Caution list — evidence sourcing

Active malignancy / retinopathy / pregnancy: class caution from somatropin labels and GH/IGF-1 biology, not DAC-specific trials.  
Glucose and edema: class GH-climate effects plus injection-site and flushing observations in Teichman.  
Sleep apnea: class pattern from GH excess / acromegaly physiology, not a DAC apnea trial.  
Pituitary failure: mechanism — no somatotrophs, no answer.  
Cardiovascular: Phase II temporal association, file never publicly closed; not a rate.

## Core biological cascade

**CJC-1295-DAC → covalent albumin bioconjugation → prolonged GHRHR occupancy on pituitary somatotrophs → increased GH secretory tone with preserved pulsatility (higher trough, waves still present) → hepatic IGF-1 rise lasting longer than the GH bump → week-to-week climate overlap if dosed weekly → systemic growth / repair climate.**

### Albumin tether is the whole point of *this* card

Native GHRH(1–29) dies in minutes (DPP-IV and other proteases). Four substitutions slow that. DAC is the week-scale move: maleimide chemistry onto albumin Cys34. Jetté 2005 is the identification paper. Teichman 2006 is the human half-life paper.

### Pulsatility is not a slogan here

Ionescu & Frohman 2006 sampled every 20 minutes overnight in healthy men, before and one week after 60 or 90 µg/kg. Pulse frequency and peak height were not the story. Basal (trough) GH rose about 7.5-fold. Mean GH +46%. IGF-1 +45%. Conclusion in the paper: pulsatility preserved; trough rise implicated in the IGF-1 increase.

That is the correction to “long-acting = flat HGH drip.” It is also the correction to “DAC makes bigger pulses.” It makes a higher floor.

### Two doors, one factory

CJC-DAC only works GHRHR. Ipamorelin works GHS-R1a. Those pathways can potentiate on the same somatotroph. That is why a pair is biologically coherent. There is still no published human RCT of CJC-1295 (either form) plus Ipamorelin. Combination magnitude in a person is unmeasured.

Insulin after food suppresses GH release. Fasted injection is physiology. Nightly timing is stronger logic for a short analog than for albumin-tethered DAC.

### What this cascade does not include

Autophagy restoration as an outcome. “25-year-old axis.” Muscle, fat, sleep, or injury repair as primary endpoints of a completed DAC program. Those may be *why someone buys it*. They were not what Teichman measured.

## Regulatory note

CJC-1295 with DAC is not an FDA-approved drug. ConjuChem’s Phase II HIV-lipodystrophy program stopped after a myocardial infarction the same day as an 11th weekly dose. The site called it old coronary disease. That file was never publicly closed. It is not a completed safety study. We do not treat the investigator line as proven. We do not treat a halted small-company trial as proof the molecule is cardiotoxic. The usable fact is: there is no completed long safety study of CJC-1295.
