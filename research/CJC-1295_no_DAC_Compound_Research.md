## Identity and evidence boundary

**Published CJC-1295** (Jetté et al., *Endocrinology* 2005;146:3052–3058. PMID 15817669. DOI 10.1210/en.2004-1286): tetrasubstituted hGRF(1–29) **plus** an Nε-maleimidopropionyl-lysine DAC that binds albumin, detectable in rat plasma beyond 72 h. That is the founding use of the name.

**Commercial “CJC-1295 no DAC”** is better identified as **Modified GRF 1-29**: the related tetrasubstituted backbone (D-Ala2, Gln8, Ala15, Leu27) **without** that C-terminal albumin-binding construction. It is not the identical finished molecule with one accessory clipped off. Gajda et al., *Drug Test Anal* 2018 (PMID 30136411) confirmed “modified GRF 1-29” as a distinct circulating commercial substance — identity, not PK. CAS often listed 863288-34-0; mass ~3368 Da.

Governing maxim: **mechanism earns inclusion; evidence type determines wording; the wrong molecule cannot borrow the right paper.**

Evidence layers that matter here — keep these separate:

1. **Direct human PK/PD on this exact four-substitution no-DAC molecule** — **not found.** No dedicated human pharmacokinetic or pharmacodynamic trial of the complete no-DAC form.
2. **Human single-substitution (D-Ala2) evidence** — real, but do not inflate it into the whole molecule.
3. **DAC data** — belongs to the sister molecule; do not import its half-life or fold-changes here.
4. **Related-molecule / class data (tesamorelin, pulsatile-GRF animal work)** — climate and pattern context, not this bottle.
5. **Practitioner / community convention** — ~100 µg bedtime start, 12/4 cycle, “gentler because the wave ends.” Keep visible; do not promote to trial-derived.

### Dose and cycling — evidence sourcing

The ~100 µg bedtime start and the 12-weeks-on / 4-weeks-off calendar are practitioner-community convention. No human dose-ranging or desensitization study on this exact molecule was identified at any dose or calendar. “Nightly forever is safe” and “the gland wears out at week twelve” are both unproven here.

### Washout — evidence sourcing

No week-scale tail to clear: without the albumin tether, exposure is short, so stopping removes the signal by the next morning rather than over days. There is no published discontinuation/rebound study on this molecule; the “no crash, no souvenir” framing follows from the short exposure, not from a dedicated washout trial.

### Human D-Ala2 evidence (do not inflate)

Soule et al., *JCEM* 1994;79:854–859. PMID 7962295. DOI 10.1210/jcem.79.4.7962295. In healthy men, D-Ala2-GHRH(1–29) during IV infusion increased disappearance half-life from approximately **4.3 to 6.7 minutes** — support for improved resistance to degradation from **one** substitution. It does not establish a 30-minute half-life for the complete four-substitution Modified GRF 1-29.

### Where “~30 minutes” sits

Community/vendor estimate, resting on design logic (four substitutions vs the unmodified fragment) and on the related short GHRH analog tesamorelin (~26–38 min in people — clock cousin, different molecule and job). Label it an estimate. Do not print it as measured fact for this vial.

### Do not import from DAC

Teichman 2006 JCEM (DAC): half-life 5.8–8.1 days; mean GH 2- to 10-fold ≥6 days; mean IGF-1 1.5- to 3-fold 9–11 days. Ionescu & Frohman 2006 (DAC): trough GH ~7.5-fold, pulse height and frequency unchanged. Those stay on the DAC card and do not describe this molecule.

### Response-tracking evidence sourcing

There is no human lab curve specific to this molecule. A rise in a downstream number (if someone already tracks one) shows the axis moved; it does not prove the reason the vial was bought. Tracking guidance in Tier 1 is this reference’s framework, not a trial protocol.

## Core biological cascade

**Modified GRF 1-29 → short-lived GHRH-receptor occupancy on pituitary somatotrophs → a single evening GH wave (no raised week-long floor) → overnight hepatic/tissue repair signaling on that night → cleared by morning, nothing carried forward.**

### Short GHRH request, no albumin tether

Native GHRH(1–29) dies in minutes to DPP-IV and other proteases. The four substitutions slow that degradation somewhat. What this molecule lacks is the DAC maleimide-to-albumin move that turns the DAC bottle into a week-scale agent. No tether means no multi-day floor — the whole behavioral difference from the weekly bottle traces to that missing tail.

### Pulse pattern matters on this receptor family

Clark RG, Robinson ICAF. *Nature* 1985;314:281–283. PMID 2858818. In rats, pulsatile IV GRF(1–29) accelerated growth and increased pituitary GH content, while continuous infusion of the same total daily dose was ineffective. This supports “pattern matters on this receptor family.” It does not prove the commercial no-DAC form needs no break — it is animal, related-molecule pattern data.

### Two doors, one factory

The GHRH receptor and the ghrelin receptor (Ipamorelin’s door) can potentiate on the same somatotroph (Cunha SR, Mayo KE. *Endocrinology* 2002. PMID 12446584. DOI 10.1210/en.2002-220670). That is why a CJC + Ipamorelin pairing is mechanistically coherent. A human trial of Modified GRF 1-29 + Ipamorelin was **not found**, so combination magnitude in a person is unmeasured.

### Related-molecule human duration (tesamorelin)

Falutz et al., *AIDS* 2008. PMID 18690162. 52 weeks of daily tesamorelin held visceral-fat reduction with no scheduled break in that trial and reversed after stopping. This is **related-molecule** support — same door family, daily pulse pattern — not this molecule, not this indication, and not a cycling exemption for Modified GRF 1-29.

### What this cascade does not include

A raised week-long GH floor (that is the DAC bottle). Bigger pulses (nothing here shows amplitude gain). Muscle, fat, sleep, or injury repair as measured endpoints of a completed trial on this molecule — those may be why someone buys it; they are not what any study of this exact form measured.
