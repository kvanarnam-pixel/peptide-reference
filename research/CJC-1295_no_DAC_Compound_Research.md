### TIER 2 — Technical (renders below the divider)

## Identity and naming boundary

**Published CJC-1295** (Jetté et al., Endocrinology 2005;146:3052–3058. PMID 15817669. DOI 10.1210/en.2004-1286): tetrasubstituted hGRF(1–29) **plus** an Nε-maleimidopropionyl-lysine DAC that binds albumin. Detectable in rat plasma beyond 72 h. That is the founding use of the name.

**Commercial “CJC-1295 no DAC”** is better identified as **Modified GRF 1-29**: the related tetrasubstituted backbone (D-Ala2, Gln8, Ala15, Leu27) **without** that C-terminal albumin-binding construction. It is not the identical finished molecule with one accessory clipped off.

Gajda et al., Drug Test Anal 2018 (PMID 30136411): “modified GRF 1-29” confirmed as a distinct circulating commercial substance. Identity, not PK.

CAS often listed 863288-34-0. Mass ~3368 Da.

## Direct human PK/PD on this exact form

**Not found.** No dedicated human pharmacokinetic or pharmacodynamic trial of the complete four-substitution no-DAC molecule.

## Human D-Ala2 evidence (do not inflate)

Soule et al., JCEM 1994;79:854–859. PMID 7962295. DOI 10.1210/jcem.79.4.7962295. In healthy men, D-Ala2-GHRH(1–29) during IV infusion increased disappearance half-life from approximately **4.3 to 6.7 minutes**. That supports improved resistance to degradation from **one** substitution. It does not establish a 30-minute half-life for complete Modified GRF 1-29.

## Where “~30 minutes” still sits

Community / vendor estimate. Design logic (four substitutions vs unmodified fragment). Related short GHRH analog tesamorelin ~26–38 min in people — clock cousin, different molecule and job.

Label as estimate. Do not print as measured fact for this vial.

## Do not import from DAC

Teichman 2006 JCEM: half-life 5.8–8.1 days; mean GH 2- to 10-fold ≥6 days; mean IGF-1 1.5- to 3-fold 9–11 days.  
Ionescu & Frohman 2006: trough GH ~7.5-fold; pulse height and frequency unchanged.

Those stay on the DAC card.

## Pulsatile vs continuous (related system, not this bottle)

Clark RG, Robinson ICAF. Nature 1985;314:281–283. PMID 2858818. Rats: pulsatile IV GRF(1–29) accelerated growth and increased pituitary GH content; continuous infusion of the same total daily dose was ineffective.

Supports “pattern matters on this receptor family.” Does not prove commercial no-DAC needs no break.

## Related-molecule human duration (tesamorelin)

Falutz et al., AIDS 2008. PMID 18690162. 52 weeks of daily tesamorelin; visceral adipose tissue reduction held with no scheduled break in that trial; reversed after stop. **Explicitly related-molecule support.** Same door family, daily pulse pattern. Not this molecule. Not this indication. Not a cycling exemption for Modified GRF 1-29.

## Two-door rationale

GHRH receptor + ghrelin receptor (Ipamorelin) can potentiate on the same somatotroph (Cunha & Mayo, Endocrinology 2002. PMID 12446584. DOI 10.1210/en.2002-220670). Mechanism coherent. Human trial of Modified GRF 1-29 + Ipamorelin: **not found.**

## Handling note

Usual multi-dose wet window ~28 days. A mixed bottle drawn every night for many weeks is longer than that window. Exact potency and sterility across that calendar have not been established. Do not assume a late draw equals an early one.
