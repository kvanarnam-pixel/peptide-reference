# BPC-157 Compound Research

Companion source of truth for the BPC-157 card. Card copy is a distillation of this file, not a replacement for it. Evidence layers follow the TB-500 template.

## Executive identity

**BPC-157 is the construction foreman.** It does not build tissue. It gets repair crews to an injured site faster and keeps the rebuild organized instead of leaving a mess.

Construction-crew family:

- **BPC-157:** foreman — local coordination, blood-supply access, organized matrix work.
- **TB-500:** scaffolding + access crew — cell mobility and systemic reach.
- **GHK-Cu:** materials and blueprints.
- **MOTS-c:** power plant.

## Evidence layers

1. **Compound-specific preclinical evidence:** rodent and cell work using BPC-157 itself (tendon fibroblast outgrowth, VEGFR2–Akt–eNOS angiogenesis, spinal-cord compression, infarct models, gut-injury models). This is the main body of the literature.
2. **Human evidence:** extremely thin. Early ulcerative-colitis interest exists in the literature; a locatable primary trial publication with methods and results is not established. No large randomized efficacy program.
3. **Mechanistic inference:** downstream organ-system claims that follow from angiogenesis, nitric-oxide restoration, and inflammation resolution rather than from a direct study in that tissue.
4. **Practical experience:** 250 mcg–1 mg/day conventions, split AM/PM dosing, 8–12 week repair runs.

Mechanism earns inclusion; evidence type determines wording. Most of the dramatic organ-system outcomes below are animal-model findings, not human trial results.

Almost all published BPC-157 work is concentrated in one research group (Sikiric / Zagreb) plus a smaller independent tendon/angiogenesis set (Chang, Hsieh). That concentration is worth naming, not glossing.

## Core cascade

**BPC-157 → VEGFR2 internalization + eNOS release (including Src–Caveolin-1) → nitric oxide / organized angiogenesis → EGR-1 recruitment of repair cells → fibroblast migration and aligned collagen.**

Inflammation is resolved rather than blocked outright: acute TNF-α / IL-6 / IL-1β signaling is allowed, then shifted toward IL-10 and context-appropriate TGF-β. That is a different job from NSAIDs.

No dedicated high-affinity receptor has been identified. It behaves as a pleiotropic modulator. That is why classic receptor-downregulation cycling arguments do not have a target to test.

| Maintenance | 250 mcg daily | Lowest common practical exposure that still produces a repair signal in use. |
| Active | 250–500 mcg daily | Typical human convention; often split AM/PM because plasma exposure is short. |

### Trial-derived vs practical

- **Trial-derived:** ~100–110 mcg/day, scaled from 10 µg/kg rodent doses.
- **Practical (what's on the card):** 250 mcg–1 mg/day, split AM/PM ~12 h apart; up to 2 mg short-term for severe acute injury; diminishing returns above 1 mg.
- **Why they diverge:** the practical range is empirical human convention, 2–5× the scaled number. Rodent milligram-per-kilogram math is a data point, not a person finding their minimum effective repair response. Use the practical range for real protocols.

BPC-157 is a cascade initiator, not a linear agonist. Once the pathways are engaged, extra peptide does not keep amplifying repair. Tissue capacity becomes the bottleneck.

## Organ-system evidence (preclinical unless noted)

- **Cardiac:** Infarct models show elimination of visible infarct damage, normalized heart-attack blood markers, and preserved pump function — same blood-flow mechanism as elsewhere.
- **Stroke:** Ischemia models report full functional recovery (motor, memory, balance) via the same blood-flow pathway.
- **Spinal cord:** Compression models reduce the injury cascade; spasticity resolves in ~2 weeks. Nerve cuts heal faster, thicker, less painful.
- **Tendon/ligament:** Strongest, most independently replicated evidence — organized collagen along force lines; healed tissue stronger than original.
- **Bone:** Heals defects that do not heal untreated, comparable to a graft. Smaller evidence base than soft tissue.
- **Kidney:** Protects the injured kidney directly, and protects kidney/liver/lungs as distant organs from injury elsewhere.
- **Gut:** Home turf — restores lining integrity, deepest evidence base, only system with real human-trial history (still thin and not a locatable modern RCT).
- **Lungs:** Real protection in general lung-injury models. COVID-specific claims are hypothesis only.
- **Mood:** Antidepressant-like effect in standard models, tied to serotonin. Not “rapid,” not proven via dopamine/HPA.
- **Metabolic:** Mechanism would predict better insulin sensitivity — reasoned extrapolation, not directly tested for this compound.

## Cancer

Genuinely unresolved. The same blood-vessel-growth mechanism behind every benefit above is exactly what oncology drugs block in existing tumors. Isolated anti-tumor observations exist in specific rodent models; they do not settle the question. No study either direction in a person with active cancer. Not a casual reach with active or recent cancer history.

This is an anchor claim, not a footnote. The card gives it equal visual weight to Bottom Line.

A mechanistic case is sometimes made that BPC-157’s angiogenesis is organized/injury-tied rather than tumor-like, and that p53 support would be anti-cancer where p53 still works. Roughly half of human cancers carry TP53 mutations. That argument does not close the file.

## PK

~15-minute plasma half-life; downstream effects last 24 h+. It triggers a cascade and does not need to stay in blood. Gut-stable (survives gastric juice). Systemic oral absorption is uncharacterized — oral use is a gut-local argument, not a systemic one.

## Cycling

No evidence of tolerance or downregulation with continuous use — and no receptor has been identified to test that. 8–12 week runs track real structural repair (organized vessels, collagen maturation) better than the shorter 4–6 week community convention. The short cycle is cost/caution management, not a biological off-switch. Stopping is a separate decision from cycling.

## Stacking

Non-overlapping mechanism with TB-500 (local build vs systemic mobilization). Complementary jobs. Combination untested, not proven.

## Supportive supplements

Vitamin C, collagen peptides, zinc carnosine, NAC, glutamine, silica, lysine/proline — the raw materials of matrix work. The peptide coordinates; it does not supply collagen amino acids or cofactors.

## Common mistakes

1. Treating the organ-system list (cardiac, stroke, spinal cord, bone, kidney, lungs) as established human outcomes. Almost all of it is rodent/preclinical work, concentrated in one research group.
2. Downplaying the cancer caution as a footnote. It shares the exact angiogenesis mechanism responsible for every benefit above — it gets equal visual weight to Bottom Line for a reason.
3. Assuming oral BPC-157 works the same as injectable. Oral is a gut-local argument (the peptide survives gastric juice); systemic oral absorption has not been characterized.
4. Treating the 4–6 week on/off cycle as biological necessity. No receptor has been identified to desensitize — the shorter community cycle is cost/caution convention, not a required reset.
5. Pushing dose past 1 mg/day expecting proportionally more repair. BPC-157 is a cascade initiator, not a linear agonist — tissue capacity, not peptide amount, becomes the bottleneck.
6. Treating the trial-derived number (~100–110 mcg/day) and the practical range (250 mcg–1 mg/day) as competing claims rather than two different, intentionally-documented things — one scaled from rodent mg/kg math, one from empirical human convention.
7. Expecting BPC-157 to supply the raw materials of repair. It coordinates the rebuild; it is not the collagen, protein, or cofactors that materials come from.
8. Treating BPC-157 and TB-500 as redundant. Non-overlapping mechanisms — local coordination vs. systemic mobilization — not two versions of the same peptide.

## What They're Not Telling You

- Foreman, not lumber. It organizes repair. It is not the collagen, the protein, the sleep, or the rehab.
- The 4–6 week on/off cycle is convention, not receptor biology.
- Split dosing matches the short plasma half-life; once-daily still works for many people.
- Oral is for the gut lining. It is not a stealth systemic route.
- “Grows new blood vessels” is the healing trick and the cancer caution. Same mechanism.
- Most of the organ-system drama is animal work from one dominant research group. Independent tendon/angiogenesis papers exist; they do not convert rodent infarct recovery into a human cardiology protocol.

## Regulatory

WADA-prohibited (S0). Not FDA-compoundable as of August 2026. FDA placed BPC-157 in 503A Category 2 in September 2023. The Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee voted 8–6–1 on July 23, 2026 to recommend it for the 503A Bulks List; that vote is nonbinding. FDA still has to accept it and finish rulemaking. Nothing is FDA-approved.
